How it works

From complaint to fix, in three steps

Mtaafix makes it easy to speak up — and hard for issues to be ignored.

1

Report the issue

Take a photo, drop a location, and describe the problem in under a minute. Works on any phone.

2

We route it to the right office

Every report is categorized and forwarded to the organization responsible — county, water, power, or roads.

3

Track it publicly

Follow your report's status in real time. Organizations respond publicly, so everyone sees progress.
